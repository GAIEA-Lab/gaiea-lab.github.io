### GAIEA Lab 🌱
---
Welcome to the GAIEA Lab, Geospatial AI for Ecosystems in the Anthropocene. We develop AI approaches for monitoring ecosystems that are ecologically grounded, globally relevant, and reliable for real-world decision-making. We do so by building methods that integrate diverse data sources to understand, predict, and support decisions in rapidly changing ecosystems. As a mentor, I strive to build a supportive, safe, and inclusive environment for my students so they have the community and the resources they need to grow into curious, independent, and interdisciplinary researchers. I am fortunate to have a wonderful group of researchers working with me towards this mission!
